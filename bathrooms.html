Can you resize a vanity I already have?+

Yes. Resizing an oversized vanity was one of the shop’s first jobs, and modifications are still part of what we do.

Why teak for a shower?+

Teak is naturally oily and holds up well to water, which makes it a good choice for duck boards that sit in the shower.

Is a single vanity too small a job?+

Not at all. Single pieces and small requests are welcome. Tell us about the room and we’ll talk it through.
