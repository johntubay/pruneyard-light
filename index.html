02 / The woodshopFrom the job site to the bench
Made inCampbell, California

A garage fix that became a woodshop.

The shop began in Rob Parfett’s garage, solving the problems off-the-shelf products couldn’t: an oversized vanity cut down to fit, a teak duck board for a custom shower.

Today it’s a dedicated workshop beside the Parfett Construction office, taking on detailed, dimension-specific work for homes in Campbell.

06 / The processOne point of contact
The process

From a first idea to the final fit.

You work directly with the people building your piece. Drawings, choices and timing stay clear, so you always know what comes next.

01/ Talk & measure

Tell us what you need, how you use the space and any photos or dimensions you have. We then measure the room and look at the details that matter.

02/ Design & materials

We work through layout, wood species and finish together, so you know exactly what will be made before work begins.

03/ Make in the shop

Your piece is built in our Campbell workshop with Festool and SawStop equipment, and you’re kept informed as it takes shape.

04/ Fit & finish

We install on site, fine-tune the fit and check the finishing details with you.

Ask a question (408) 429-8713
Where are you based?+

Our woodshop is in Campbell, right beside the Parfett Construction office. Most of our cabinetry work is for homes in and around Campbell.

Can you modify cabinets I already have?+

Yes. Alongside new cabinetry, we can modify existing cabinets and resize pieces that don’t quite fit, like a vanity that’s too large for the room.

What wood do you work with?+

Solid wood is our focus, and white oak is a favourite for clean, modern interiors. We don’t specialise in laminate or Formica work.

Can you build a hidden bookshelf door?+

Yes. Concealed bookshelf doors are one of our specialities: a working bookshelf that swings open to a room or closet behind it.

Do you handle larger renovations too?+

Pruneyard Cabinets is the woodshop of Parfett Construction Inc., a Bay Area general contractor. If your project goes beyond cabinetry, we can bring in the Parfett team.
