04 / The processOne point of contact
The process

From a first idea to the final fit.

You work directly with the people building your piece. Drawings, choices and timing stay clear, so you always know what comes next.

01/ Talk & measure

Tell us what you need, how you use the space and any photos or dimensions you have. We then measure the room and look at the details that matter.

02/ Design & materials

We work through layout, wood species and finish together, so you know exactly what will be made before work begins.

03/ Make in the shop

Your piece is built in our Campbell workshop with Festool and SawStop equipment, and you’re kept informed as it takes shape.

04/ Fit & finish

We install on site, fine-tune the fit and check the finishing details with you.
